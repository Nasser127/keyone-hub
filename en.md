## What is KeyOne Hub?

KeyOne Hub is an open-source, modular ecosystem built to give users full control over their own hardware.
It is designed to bypass the artificial software limitations imposed by manufacturers—without breaking security, rooting, or jailbreaking.

Whether you're using an iPad, smartphone, tablet, or wearable device, KeyOne Hub provides a universal layer of control
that lets you choose, install, and run any operating system on your device—freely and safely.

This platform is more than just a tool—it's a gateway to freedom of choice in a world of locked systems.
